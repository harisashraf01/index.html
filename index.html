My Dearest Nazira, ❤️

Happy Birthday, meri jaan! 🥹

Aaj tumhara din hai, aur main chahta hoon ke tum ek baat dil se jaano: tum meri zindagi mein bohot special ho.

Jab main hamare saath bitaye hue pal yaad karta hoon, toh university library ke woh moments, tumhare saath gaadi mein ghoomna, hamari lambi baatein, hasi mazaak aur hamari chhoti chhoti ladaiyan sab yaad aati hain.

Kuch yaadein humein hansati hain, kuch dil ko emotional kar deti hain, aur kuch yeh yaad dilati hain ke kisi insaan ki presence hamare liye kitni important ho sakti hai.

Main yeh nahi kehta ke hamari zindagi hamesha perfect hogi. Kabhi hum disagree karenge, kabhi ek doosre ko samajhne mein waqt lagega. Lekin meri khwahish hai ke hum gusse se zyada patience, ego se zyada understanding aur har mushkil mein respect ko choose karein.

Meri jaan, tumhari birthday par meri dua hai ke Allah tumhari zindagi khushiyon, sehat, kamyabi aur sukoon se bhar de. Tumhare dreams poore hon, tumhari smile hamesha bani rahe, aur tumhe hamesha woh pyaar aur respect mile jiski tum haqdar ho. Ameen. 🤲🏻

Main tumhare saath aur khoobsurat memories banana chahta hoon. Aur agar tum bhi yahi chaho, toh main chahta hoon ke hum apne future ko saath milkar build karein — ek doosre ke dreams ko support karte hue, mushkil waqt mein baat karte hue, aur har din ek doosre ko choose karte hue.

Tum mere liye sirf aaj ki khushi nahi ho. Tum woh insaan ho jiske saath main aane wale kal ke khoobsurat possibilities imagine karta hoon.

Happy Birthday, my special Nazira. ❤️

Tumhari zindagi hamesha roshan rahe, tumhare dil ko sukoon mile aur tumhari har nayi subah ek nayi umeed laaye.

With all my heartfelt wishes,
